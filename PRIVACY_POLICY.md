# Privacy Policy for Tamil Radio Discord Bot

**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how **Tamil Radio & News Discord Bot** ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects user data when you interact with the Bot.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Automatically Collected Discord Data
To function correctly, the Bot interacts with standard Discord API data:
- **Server (Guild) ID & Voice Channel ID**: Required to connect the Bot to your voice channel and manage auto-join / auto-disconnect logic.
- **User ID & Username**: Temporarily processed when you run slash commands (`/play`, `/stop`, etc.) or join/leave a voice channel.

### B. Audio & Voice Data
- **No Voice Recording**: The Bot **DOES NOT** record, save, store, or monitor any human voice or audio transmitted by users in Discord voice channels.
- The Bot only transmits outward live audio streams from external public radio streams into the voice channel.

## 2. How We Use Collected Information
We use the processed data solely for:
- Connecting the Bot to designated voice channels.
- Responding to slash commands issued by users.
- Automatically disconnecting when a voice channel is empty to conserve network bandwidth and computing resources.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention
- **No Persistent Database Storage**: The Bot **does not store** personal user data, chat histories, or messages in any external database.
- Temporary runtime variables (e.g., active voice connection states) exist only in volatile server memory while the Bot is running and are cleared when the Bot restarts or disconnects.

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **Discord API**: The Bot operates through Discord's official Gateway and API. Use of Discord is subject to [Discord’s Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Public Radio Streams**: The Bot streams audio from third-party public audio streams. No user data is transmitted to these third-party stream sources.

## 5. Third-Party Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Bot complies with Discord's Terms of Service and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age limit).

## 7. Data Removal & Requests
Since the Bot does not persistently store user data, there is no personal data stored to delete. However, if you wish to stop data processing for your server, simply remove (kick) the Bot from your Discord server.

## 8. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this document with an updated "Effective Date".

## 9. Contact Us
If you have any questions or privacy concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the GitHub repository or contact the Bot owner on Discord.
